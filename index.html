01 — ENGINEERING CASE STUDY

Making pipeline failures
easier to understand.

Developed components of an AI-assisted failure analysis system that brings Databricks and CloudWatch logs together, retrieves relevant knowledge, and gives engineers useful resolution recommendations.

Step FunctionsLambdaS3Bedrock

The problem

Failure context is spread across job logs and monitoring systems, making investigation repetitive.

The approach

Collect and structure logs in S3, search a knowledge base for known errors, use an LLM for additional suggestions, and notify an engineer. Recommendations require review before production changes.

My contribution

Pipeline monitoring, log aggregation, knowledge retrieval and notification components that help engineers investigate failures with relevant context.
